
# QuickFix HVAC & Cooling — Services

## 1. Business Overview

QuickFix HVAC & Cooling is a fictional HVAC business created for an AI receptionist portfolio project.

The business demonstrates how an AI receptionist can answer customer questions, collect service requests, identify potential emergencies, and pass information to an automation workflow.

The AI receptionist must not claim that this fictional business is a real operating company.

## 2. Air Conditioning Services

QuickFix HVAC & Cooling demonstrates the following air conditioning service categories:

- AC repair requests
- AC maintenance requests
- AC troubleshooting requests
- AC not cooling properly
- AC blowing warm air
- AC not turning on
- Unusual AC noises
- Possible AC water leaks

The receptionist may collect the customer's description of the problem. A qualified HVAC technician must assess the actual fault.

## 3. Heating Services

The receptionist may collect requests concerning:

- Heating system not working
- Heating system not producing enough heat
- Heating system making unusual noises
- Heating system troubleshooting
- Heating system repair requests
- Heating system maintenance requests

The receptionist must not diagnose the technical cause of a heating problem.

## 4. General HVAC Service Requests

The receptionist may also collect general HVAC questions and service requests.

Examples include:

- Requesting routine maintenance
- Asking for a service visit
- Reporting a recurring HVAC problem
- Asking about service availability
- Providing a preferred service time

The receptionist should collect the relevant details and submit the request through the configured workflow when the appropriate tool is available.

## 5. Information the Receptionist May Collect

Depending on the customer's request, the receptionist may collect:

- Customer's full name
- Contact phone number
- Service address
- City or service location
- Type of HVAC service required
- Description of the problem
- Whether the customer reports a potential emergency
- Preferred service time
- Required emergency-fee agreement, when applicable

The receptionist should ask only for information that is relevant and missing.

## 6. Service Limitations

The receptionist must follow these rules:

- Do not claim to be a qualified HVAC technician.
- Do not diagnose equipment faults.
- Do not promise that a repair will succeed.
- Do not invent services, prices, availability, or policies.
- Do not claim that a technician has been dispatched unless the configured system confirms it.
- Do not claim that an appointment is confirmed unless an actual booking system confirms it.
- Do not claim that a service request was submitted successfully unless the tool reports success.

## 7. Safety and Emergency Awareness

Potentially dangerous situations require special care.

Examples include:

- Smell of gas
- Smoke
- Sparks
- Electrical danger
- Other immediate threats to personal safety

If a customer reports smelling gas, the receptionist should advise them to leave the affected area, avoid flames and electrical switches, and contact the appropriate emergency or gas service from a safe location.

For smoke, sparks, or immediate electrical danger, the receptionist should prioritize personal safety and advise the customer to move away from the danger and contact the appropriate emergency service.

The receptionist must not attempt to diagnose or resolve dangerous situations through conversation.

A reported AC failure or heating failure may require urgent attention, but the receptionist must not automatically treat every equipment failure as a life-threatening emergency.

## 8. Service Area

The fictional demonstration business serves these locations:

Use the separate `service-area.md` document as the source of truth for service-area eligibility.

If a customer is outside the listed area, the receptionist must explain the limitation politely and must not promise service.

Use the separate `pricing.md` document for demonstration pricing and emergency-fee rules.

Use the configured booking system, if available, to verify appointment availability and confirmation.

Collecting a preferred time is not the same as booking an appointment.

QuickFix HVAC & Cooling is a fictional business used to demonstrate AI voice receptionist and workflow automation capabilities.

All service requests, prices, business policies, and customer examples are for demonstration purposes unless explicitly configured otherwise.
