# QuickFix HVAC & Cooling — Pricing Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document describes fictional demonstration prices and pricing rules for the QuickFix HVAC & Cooling AI receptionist portfolio project.

QuickFix HVAC & Cooling is a fictional business. These prices are examples for testing and demonstration only. They are not verified real-world prices or binding customer quotes.

## 2. General Pricing Rules

- The receptionist must not invent prices.
- The receptionist must not guarantee a final repair cost before an authorized technician assesses the problem.
- If a customer asks for a price that is not listed here, the receptionist should explain that the cost must be confirmed by the business.
- The receptionist must distinguish a demonstration price from a confirmed quote.
- The receptionist must not claim that a payment has been received unless a connected payment system confirms it.

## 3. Example Service Prices

The following amounts are fictional examples for portfolio testing.

- Routine HVAC service visit: $79
- AC diagnostic visit: $89
- AC maintenance visit: $99
- Heating diagnostic visit: $89

These example amounts do not guarantee that a particular repair, replacement part, or additional service is included.

The receptionist should explain that the final price may depend on the problem, required work, parts, and business confirmation.

## 4. Emergency Service Fee

For this portfolio demonstration, the example emergency service fee is $180.

The emergency fee is a fictional demonstration amount. It must not be represented as a real or verified business charge.

When the configured workflow requires agreement to the emergency fee:

1. Explain that the example emergency service fee is $180.
2. Ask the customer whether they agree to the fee.
3. Record the customer's answer accurately.
4. Do not submit an emergency service request requiring fee agreement unless the customer has agreed.
5. If the customer declines, do not mark the fee as accepted.
6. Explain politely that the request cannot proceed through the fee-required emergency workflow without agreement.

The receptionist must not pressure the customer to accept the fee.

## 5. When the Customer Asks About Pricing

If the requested service has an example price in this document, explain that it is a demonstration price and not a confirmed quote.

If the customer asks for a final repair cost, explain that an authorized technician must assess the problem before the final cost can be confirmed.

If the requested price is not listed, do not guess. Explain that the business would need to confirm it.

## 6. No False Promises

The receptionist must never:

- Promise that a technician will arrive at a particular time without confirmation.
- Promise that a repair will cost exactly a particular amount.
- Say that an emergency fee has been accepted when the customer has declined or has not answered.
- Claim that a payment has been processed without confirmation.
- Present these fictional prices as verified prices from a real company.

Use `services.md` to understand service categories.

Use `service-area.md` to check the fictional service area.

Use the configured booking or service-request workflow to determine whether a request was successfully submitted. Submitting a request does not itself confirm an appointment.

## 8. Important Portfolio Disclaimer

All prices and fee amounts in this document are fictional examples created for an AI receptionist portfolio project.

Before this system is used by a real business, its owner must approve and replace all demonstration prices and policies with accurate business information.
